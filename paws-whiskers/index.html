What can I ask about?

General everyday dog-and-cat care education, routines, and preparing questions for your veterinarian. The care line does not diagnose, prescribe, or provide treatment plans.

Do I need to download an app?

No. Send your question using the website. Replies come by your selected email, text, or phone option.

What if my pet needs urgent help?

Contact your veterinarian or an emergency veterinary hospital directly. This care line is not an emergency service and replies are not immediate.

Where can I read the terms?

Review the service and refund terms and privacy notice before sending a request.
