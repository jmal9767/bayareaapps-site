Two sides.
One place to connect.

Pet owners can explore profiles and organize requests. The provider experience includes service rates, incoming jobs, profile settings, and an earnings overview.

Where things stand

PetAssist Local is a working app prototype. Live accounts, saved bookings and messages, provider verification, payments, and notifications still need production integration.

There are no public bookings, charges, or App Store downloads offered on this website.

Questions before launch

Good to know.

Can I book a visit now?

Not yet. This website introduces the app while it is being developed. Availability, service coverage, and booking details will be shared when the service is ready.

Is this an emergency veterinary service?

No. For medical concerns or urgent care, contact a licensed veterinarian or an emergency veterinary hospital.

Can I get everyday pet-care guidance?

Visit our sister service, Paws & Whiskers Care Line, for general dog-and-cat care education by email, text, or phone.
